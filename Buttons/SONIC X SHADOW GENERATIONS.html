SONIC X SHADOW GENERATIONS

ABOUT THE GAME

Shadow teams up with Sonic in SONIC X SHADOW GENERATIONS!

Prove that Shadow is the ultimate life form! Rush through important places from his past and defeat Black Doom.

SONIC X SHADOW GENERATIONS includes SONIC GENERATIONS with enhanced graphics.

RETURN OF SHADOW

In the new story campaign, your opponent will be Black Doom, Shadow's sworn enemy, who will once again try to take over the world. Shadow will have to go back in time, relive tragic events and master new powers to save the universe.

EVOLUTION OF SHADOW

Overcome hordes of enemies and a variety of challenges with the power of evil fate that Shadow has discovered within himself. Rush across the water, fly over obstacles, and stop time with the power of Chaos Control!
